ABOUT:
Courbe Sans is my first typeface, using and refining characters
I'd previously designed for a wordmark. 
Courbe Sans is a heavy, aggressive, geometric display typeface (Uppercase-only),
supporting both Latin and Greek character sets.
This typeface is in no way perfectly made, I'd appreciate any feedback!

*The .otf seems very badly spaced on the Windows Font Viewer due to a glitch
but the font itself works perfectly fine in any software*

CONTENTS:
- CourbeSans (.otf, .ttf)

HOW TO INSTALL:
1. Open the "CourbeSans" folder.
2. Double-click the font files and click "Install".
3. Restart your design software (Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, etc.).
